## About the Collection

Welcome to the magical world of beloved children’s book author and illustrator Catherine James. An alumnus of PSD and a native Philadelphian, James spent nearly 4 decades wowing children and parents alike with her beautifully sensitive mixed-media collage. In this exhibit you’ll find early drafts of her most famous projects as well as letters, diaries and photographs that helped to inspire them.
Tunnel with the ants, work up your courage with Chickie, take a walk around the park and enjoy a peaceful evening under the blue moon!
